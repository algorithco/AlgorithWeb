# Code of conduct

We want AlgorithWeb to be a place where people can ask, propose and disagree
without being treated badly.

**Do**

- Be respectful. Argue about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean in plain words.
- Accept that people have different languages, experience and time zones.
- Help newcomers.

**Do not**

- Harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone.
- Post other people's private information.
- Spam, or push unrelated promotion in issues and pull requests.
- Share sexual or violent content.

**What happens**

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block people who break these rules. If something happened to you or you saw it,
write privately to <hello@algorithco.com>. We keep reports confidential and look at them
fairly.

This code applies in the repository, in its issues and pull requests, and
wherever you speak for the project.
