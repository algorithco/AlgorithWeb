# Names and logos

The names **Algorithco**, **AlgorithVoice** and **AlgorithGuard**, and the
logos and marks that go with them, belong to Algorithco. The
[AlgorithWeb license](LICENSE) covers the source code. It does not give you any
right to use our names or marks.

You may:

- Refer to Algorithco or its products in an article, a review, a video, a talk
  or a comparison.
- Show that your tool, guide or integration works with AlgorithVoice or
  AlgorithGuard.
- List AlgorithVoice or AlgorithGuard among the tools you use.
- Say that your project is a fork of AlgorithWeb, in plain text.

You may not:

- Use a mark or a name in your own logo, product name, app icon, domain or
  social handle. A fork needs its own name and its own logo.
- Suggest that we made, sponsor or endorse something we did not.
- Put a mark on things you sell or give away, such as clothing or stickers,
  without asking first.
- Show a mark or a name beside content that is unlawful or meant to deceive.

The official brand files and the full guidelines are at
<https://trqsh.uz/brand>. For anything they do not cover, write to
<hello@algorithco.com>.
